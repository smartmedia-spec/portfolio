A little about me

Where creativity

A multidisciplinary approach to communication, creative production and technology education.

My teaching experience includes work with learners in different schools and institutions, including Strathmore University. I enjoy developing practical, learner-friendly experiences that connect programming and electronics to real-world problem-solving.
